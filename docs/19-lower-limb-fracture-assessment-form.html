Glossary of difficult terms (16)

Dotted-underlined words also show the definition on hover or tap.

  • capsule — The fibrous sleeve that surrounds and stabilises a joint.
  • contracture — A permanent tightening or shortening of tissue that restricts movement.
  • cryotherapy — Treatment using cold.
  • effusion — Fluid collecting inside a joint.
  • gait — The manner of walking.
  • inflammation — The body's protective response to injury - heat, redness, swelling and pain.
  • isometric — A muscle contraction with no change in length (no joint movement).
  • manual muscle testing — Grading a muscle's strength by resisting a voluntary contraction (abbreviated MMT).
  • oedema — Swelling caused by fluid trapped in the tissues.
  • palpation — Examining the body by touch to feel for tenderness, swelling or tissue change.
  • proprioception — The sense of body and joint position and movement.
  • range of motion — How far a joint can move (abbreviated ROM).
  • ROM — Short for range of motion - how far a joint can move.
  • TENS — Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation - a mild current passed through the skin to relieve pain.
  • torque — A turning force around an axis - the product of force and its moment arm.
  • traction — A gentle pulling force applied to a joint or the spine to relieve pressure.
